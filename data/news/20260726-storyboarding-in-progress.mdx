# Storyboarding in Progress: From Screenplay Notes to an Animatic

Storyboarding has started for the full film. The current pass moves from screenplay-derived visual notes into thumbnails, rough grayscale boards, and the first timing experiments.

## From screenplay to images

Each sequence starts with the screenplay, followed by visual notes and references selected manually by the director. The notes establish space, rhythm, staging, and the practical questions that need to be solved in drawings. The symbols, motifs, transitions, and curated references are shown below.

The experiment clarified the role of AI in the process. AI is a production tool. It cannot replace artists. A coherent sequence depends on artists who can design and maintain the background, character construction, costume, staging, and screen direction. As these tools become faster and more accessible, foundational knowledge matters even more because artists still have to judge, correct, and connect every image into a coherent film.

## Next: grayscale boards and CT

The next goal is to finish grayscale storyboards for the whole film, add temporary voices, and build a CT, commonly called an animatic in Western production. Watching the rough film end to end will let us revise timing, pacing, and cuts before Layout, Genga, Douga, and Shiage.

New tools can make individual tasks faster. They still depend on the foundational knowledge required to draw, stage, animate, and maintain a coherent screen world. That knowledge remains the production base.

# 絵コンテ制作中：脚本のメモからCTへ

この検証で、制作工程におけるAIの位置づけが明確になりました。AIは制作のための道具であり、作り手の代わりにはなりません。一貫したシークエンスには、背景、人物の構造、衣装、芝居、画面方向を設計し、維持できる作り手が必要です。ツールが速く身近になるほど、各画面を判断し、修正し、一つの映画としてつなぐための基礎力は、これまで以上に重要になります。

## 次はグレースケール絵コンテとCT

次の目標は、全編のグレースケール絵コンテを完成させ、仮の声を当ててCTを組むことです。海外では一般にアニマティックと呼ばれる工程です。映画全体をラフな状態で通して観ることで、レイアウト、原画、動画、仕上げへ進む前に、間、テンポ、カットを調整できます。

新しいツールで個別の作業を速くできても、構図、芝居、作画、背景、連続性を判断する基礎力は制作の土台として残ります。
